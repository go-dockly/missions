# Gorgopotamos

Railway viaduct in a wooded gorge. Reference: drone flights by day, ground walks and train footage. Dataset: `dataset/` (see the root README).

## Zones

Rough coverage targets for selected images, so the bridge doesn't dominate. These are conceptual zones, not measured boundaries.

| Zone | Covers | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bridge | structure, piers, abutments, tracks | 20% |
| Northern gorge | rock faces, slopes, vegetation | 20% |
| Southern gorge | riverbanks, approaches, terrain | 20% |
| Western approach | paths, forest, high ground | 15% |
| Eastern approach | access routes, surrounding terrain | 15% |
| Riverbed | boulders, banks, water course | 10% |
